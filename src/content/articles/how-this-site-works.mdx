你现在读到的不是一篇文章。它是一个**超媒介**——文字只是它的最低档位。

让我从一个简单的观察开始：信息媒介是有档位的。

## 第一档：文字

文字是最古老、也最抽象的媒介。我写下「远处有一座山，山后是落日」，你只能靠想象去补全画面。每个读者脑中的山都不一样——这既是文字的浪漫，也是它的局限。

## 第四档：可交互的软件

这是这个网站存在的理由。**比视频更高一级的媒介，是你可以亲手操作的软件。**

## 所以，这里分享什么

我做软件，也旅行、读书、录音、拍照。以前这些东西被迫散落在不同平台：文章发博客，视频传 YouTube，代码放 GitHub，各自孤立。

现在它们住进同一个容器：

- **能用文字讲清楚的**，就安安静静用文字（比如我的 [PKM 实践](/articles/pkm-method)）
- **需要看见的**，给你图像和视频
- **值得亲手体验的**，直接给你软件

欢迎来到我的超媒体主页。去 [项目](/projects) 看看我做的软件，或者去 [组件试验场](/lab) 把每个媒介组件玩个遍。

这个网站会像软件一样持续加功能——因为它就是一个软件。

What you are reading is not an article. It is **hypermedia** — and prose is only its lowest gear.

A simple observation to start: information media have levels.

## Level 1: Text

Text is the oldest medium, and the most abstract. I write “there is a mountain in the distance, and a sunset behind it,” and you have to fill in the picture. Every reader’s mountain is different — the romance of text, and its limit.

## Level 2: Image

An image lets you **see**. You no longer have to imagine. Drag the divider below and look at the gap between two media levels:

This is why the site exists. **One level above video is software you can put your hands on.**

## So what is shared here

I make software, and I travel, read, record, take photos. Those things used to be scattered: articles on a blog, video on YouTube, code on GitHub, each isolated.

Now they live in one container:

- **What prose can carry**, stays quiet prose (for example my [PKM practice](/articles/pkm-method))
- **What needs to be seen**, gets image and video
- **What is worth operating**, gets software

Welcome to the hypermedia home page. Look at the software on [Projects](/projects), or play every media component in the [lab](/lab).

This site will keep gaining features the way software does — because it is software.
